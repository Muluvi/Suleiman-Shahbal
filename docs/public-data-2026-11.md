## 7. Five numbers to use first

1. **807 votes:** Nassir's 2022 margin in Nyali, the county's second-largest constituency. With Kisauni (1,046), the two biggest constituencies were decided by about 1,850 votes.
2. **34%:** 2022 governor turnout. About 420,000 registered voters stayed home.
3. **98%** of Mombasa's pending bills are over three years old, the highest share in Kenya (CoB).
4. **55%** of county revenue goes on wages, against a 35% legal ceiling, and salaries were paid with a KSh 620 m overdraft in June 2026 (CoB, secondary).
5. **52%** of residents have basic drinking water, in a fully urban county (KDHS 2022). Mwache treated water is unlikely to arrive before the 2027 vote, because its treatment plant only starts in December 2026.
