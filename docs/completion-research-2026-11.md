## A. Public records

### A1. Form 37C, Mombasa governor 2022: ⚠️ partial
- **Not online.** IEBC posted some counties' Forms 37C as press releases in August 2022 (for example Narok), but no Mombasa 37C was found.
- **What settles the 98,105 vs 98,108 question meanwhile:** the six constituency tallies add up to 98,108 (already on the site).
- **Action:** request it in writing from the IEBC Mombasa County Returning Officer, or download it from the [IEBC press releases page](https://iebc.or.ke/media/?press).

### A2 / A3. Forms 37A/37B, governor 2013, 2017, 2022: ❌ not public in usable form
- The 2022 Forms 37A were uploaded to the IEBC results portal as scanned images ([forms.iebc.or.ke](https://forms.iebc.or.ke)). Nobody publishes them as data. They must be downloaded and typed in: **1,041 polling stations** for 2022.
- No public copies were found for 2013 and 2017. The election-court judgments recite totals only ([2013 petition](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/kehc/2013/2189/eng@2013-09-27)).
- **Usable 2022 ward-level proxy (✅ found):** MCA winners by ward are published (Mzalendo, from the IEBC gazette). Examples: Mikindani 6,988; Kongowea (UDA) 5,064; Tudor (UDA) 4,587; Freretown 4,534. Source: [Mzalendo county profile](https://www.mzalendo.com/mps-performance/counties/mombasa/). These show UDA ward wins in Nyali (Kongowea) and Mvita (Tudor), useful for the north and anchor zones.
- **Action:** a data-entry job from the IEBC portal images. Budget about 3–4 days of typing for 2022 alone.

### A4. Certified 2027 register: ⚠️ partial
- **ECVR Phase 1 final, Mombasa: 51,286 new voters** (30 Mar – 28 Apr 2026). This is now confirmed by [AllAfrica / Capital FM, 1 May 2026](https://allafrica.com/stories/202605010116.html), not only the aggregator used before. National total: 2,345,476.
- The 41,444 figure reported in late April was the count at **23 April**. It is not the final.
- KNA later reported **over 2.6 million** new voters nationally for Phase I ([KNA, Sep 2026](https://www.kenyanews.go.ke/iebc-registers-2-6m-new-voters/)). The national total was revised upward after verification, so Mombasa's figure may also rise. No updated county row was found.
- **The IEBC's own county tables** for each update are annexed to its press releases, for example the [9 April 2026 release](https://www.iebc.or.ke/uploads/resources/WDHz5ctC1r.pdf).
- **The certified register is not published yet.** IEBC gazettes it before the election.

### A16. Typhoid and disease surveillance: ⚠️ partial
- **No Mombasa typhoid counts are published.**
- Kenya: about 109,000–126,000 typhoid cases a year; typhoid vaccine added to routine immunisation in 2025 ([WHO Afro](https://www.afro.who.int/node/22070)).
- **Other Mombasa outbreaks, 2025 (✅):**
  - **Mpox:** 98 of Kenya's 226 cases and 2 deaths, mostly in Nyali and Changamwe ([Citizen, Jul 2025](https://citizen.digital/article/mpox-two-people-dead-as-mombasa-intensifies-surveillance-amid-rising-cases-n366156))
  - **Chikungunya:** 25 confirmed ([Standard, Jun 2025](https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/business/amp/coast/article/2001520927/25-cases-of-chikungunya-confirmed-in-mombasa))
  - **Cholera** cases reported in April 2025 ([WHO sitrep](https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-09/WeeK%2017%20Situation%20Report_0.pdf))
- Mombasa is among **14 counties holding half of Kenya's zero-dose (never vaccinated) children** ([Standard](https://www.standardmedia.co.ke/health/health-science/article/2001524317/more-children-receive-measles-typhoid-vaccines-as-drive-ends)).
