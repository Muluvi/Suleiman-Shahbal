## 14.0 Securing the ballot line

*   **The clock:** from the go-ahead to the IEBC's party deadlines in 2027 (certified membership lists by 23 March, nominations from 3 June), then to the 10 August 2027 general election.
*   **Success:** a valid nomination on The New Democrats' ticket, backed by organised ground reach and trained agents across all six constituencies; then at least **120,000 votes** at the ballot box, the benchmark Section 2.5 sets from Governor Nassir's 2022 winning total of 119,083.

**The two deadlines:**

1.  **The party and nomination deadlines, March–June 2027:** <window-chip></window-chip> The New Democrats' certified membership list by 23 March 2027 and the governor nomination from 3 to 6 June 2027 (Gazette Notice 13501). Success means a valid nomination and organised reach in the 353,090-voter pool (Kisauni, Nyali and Changamwe), where the 2022 race was closest. That turnout and machinery, not recognition, is his gap there is the working hypothesis Section 7.1 states and Week 1 tests, not a measured fact.
2.  **The general election, 10 August 2027:** turning out the 120,000-vote coalition a plurality win needs in a multi-candidate field.

**The record is the one to put first.** The plan argues that what he has built, Gulf African Bank and the Jomvu SEZ, and his EALA role are his differentiators. The first check is whether they appear on the profile where a voter would look for them.

**Is the Facebook presence a Page, or a personal profile in professional mode?** It is settled first (assumption 5, Section 7.4). The distinction is not cosmetic. A personal profile has no Page Insights, no Business Suite export, no delegated admin roles and no ad account — which means most of what follows does not exist, and the strategy has a migration question attached to it before anything else is decided. One screenshot of Meta Business Suite, or of the Professional dashboard, settles this in the first hour of Week 1, and it gates the rest of the audit.

Coalitions are built around **shared economic and civic interests**, in a sequence that runs from professional associations in the nomination window to public endorsements before the general election. Every endorsement is voluntary, documented and disclosed.

## 16.1 The principle behind the order

```lead
Coalitions are built around **shared economic and civic interests** — water, credit, market access, employment, transparency. They are never built around ethnic, clan or religious bloc identity.
```

Coalitions are built around **shared economic and civic interests** — water,
credit, market access, employment, transparency. They are never built around
ethnic, clan or religious bloc identity. Faith and community networks are
engaged as **civic institutions with legitimate service-delivery concerns**,
which is both the ethical position and the effective one: a water commitment
made to a congregation is a policy conversation, while a bloc appeal is a
liability.
