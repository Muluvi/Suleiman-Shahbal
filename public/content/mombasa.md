Mombasa is small, crowded, young and urban: **1,208,333** people on **220 km²**, a median age of **24.0**, and **31.8%** in poverty in 2021 (Tier 1, KNBS 2019 Census; Tier 2, KNBS poverty estimate via Stats Kenya). It is Kenya's second city and its port, producing about **5.1%** of national output (Tier 1, KNBS Gross County Product). Its next governor runs a budget of **KSh 18.73 billion** a year (Tier 3, FY2025/26, unconfirmed). This chapter sets out who lives here and how the county is run; Chapter 3 sets out what they need.

## 1.1 Who lives here

The 2019 census counted **1,208,333** people: 610,257 men, 598,046 women and 30 intersex people (Tier 1, KNBS). Mombasa is one of only two Kenyan counties with more men than women, a mark of a port city that draws working men from the rest of the coast and up-country. A 2025 population projection is not yet in hand; the record that would settle it is the KNBS county projection series.

**Age.** The median age is **24.0** (Tier 1, calculated from KNBS 2019 Census Volume III). The 2019 census puts **32.6%** of residents at 0–14, **41.8%** at 15–34, **22.4%** at 35–59 and **3.2%** at 60 and over. Of the 756,906 adults, **59.1%** are aged 18 to 34 (Tier 1, KNBS 2019 Census Volume III).

**What it means for the campaign.** A county where six adults in ten are under 35 is decided by whether young people register and turn out. They are also the most online: **70.4%** of Mombasa's young people use the internet (Tier 1, CA/KNBS 2023/24).

## 1.2 The land and its three zones

Mombasa covers **220 km²**, the second-smallest county in Kenya, at **5,495** people per km² (Tier 1, KNBS 2019 Census). It is classed entirely urban (Tier 2, CRA county fact sheet). Its six constituencies share their names and boundaries with its six census sub-counties, so in Mombasa the census and the register can be read side by side.

## 1.4 How the county is run

Mombasa has six constituencies, 30 county assembly wards and 6 sub-counties (Tier 1, IEBC and KNBS). Unlike most counties, its sub-counties and constituencies are the same six areas, so a sub-county census figure can be read against a constituency register figure directly (Section 1.2).

The Governor of Mombasa heads the county executive. Under the County Governments Act the office runs public services for more than one million residents. Its main responsibilities:
